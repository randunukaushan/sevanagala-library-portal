# Monaragala District Library Expansion Research

## 1. Scope and Confidence

This first pass transcribes the Monaragala entries in the NLDSB List of Graded Public Libraries in Sri Lanka, Uva Province section labelled 2022, and checks public digital-catalogue evidence. The list contains 24 entries: 1 Grade I, 5 Grade II, and 18 Grade III. The grade dates are historical grading dates, not proof of a library's current 2026 grade.

Public website/LMS checks were not completed library-by-library for every institution. UNKNOWN means no verified public evidence in this pass; it does not mean a system or service is absent. The National Virtual Union Catalogue currently lists Bibile among participating libraries. No claim is made here that the other 23 are not connected.

## 3. Digital Readiness Snapshot

### 3.1 Verified

- Bibile Public Library has a public Koha OPAC under the DLP domain. Its OPAC describes an ICTA/NLDSB DLP implementation in 2021, and sampled item records expose copy-level locations, call numbers, barcodes and availability.
- Bibile is named in the National Virtual Union Catalogue's participating-library list.
- The Uva Provincial Library website has a Monaragala District Library service page describing sections and services, but it does not itself verify an LMS or OPAC.

### 3.2 UNKNOWN

For the remaining 23 roster entries, public evidence for current Koha/LMS, OPAC, online membership, holds, renewals, item status, barcode/RFID, multilingual search and digital resources has not been verified in this first pass. Current grades for every entry also require confirmation from NLDSB/local authorities.

### 3.3 Readiness Classification

No library should be assigned Level A–E solely from its grade. Bibile has public evidence for catalogue discovery and item-level holdings (evidence toward Level B) and public descriptions of Koha automation/hold capability (partial evidence toward Level C); current operation, authenticated services and institutional support must still be checked. Other entries remain unclassified pending evidence.

## 4. Sevanagala Verification Questions

Before choosing a standalone circulation database or integration, ask the authorized Thanamalvila PS/library contact:

1. Is Koha or another LMS used at Sevanagala, Thanamalvila, or the local authority?
2. Which system currently holds the book catalogue, accession numbers, members and issue/return history?
3. Is any DLP or VUC enrollment planned or already active but not publicly discoverable?
4. Who owns the data, administers staff accounts and approves integrations?
5. Can the library provide an approved catalogue export or demonstration account without exposing real member data?
6. Which written rules govern membership, borrowing limits, loan periods, renewals, overdue handling, retention, and child accounts?

Do not migrate real member or circulation records until authority, lawful purpose, access, retention, backup and recovery are documented.

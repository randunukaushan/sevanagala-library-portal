# Donor Research and Outreach Strategy

## 1. Purpose

### 1.1 Objective

Define how the project identifies, qualifies, approaches, follows up with, and tracks suitable donors and partners.

## 2. Donor Segments

### 2.1 Book Donation Organisations

Target organisations that support:

- public libraries;
- rural libraries;
- schools and educational communities;
- international book distribution.

### 2.2 Education Foundations

Potential support:

- books;
- digital access;
- literacy programmes;
- learning resources;
- student support.

### 2.3 Technology Companies

Potential support:

- computers;
- networking;
- printers;
- displays;
- software;
- training.

### 2.4 Corporate CSR Programmes

Potential support:

- furniture;
- infrastructure;
- computer labs;
- Wi-Fi;
- digital-learning projects.

### 2.5 Diaspora and Community Associations

Potential support:

- targeted book drives;
- equipment;
- shipping;
- project sponsorship.

## 3. Donor Qualification

### 3.1 Eligibility

Confirm:

- country eligibility;
- organisation type accepted;
- public-library eligibility;
- application window;
- shipping restrictions;
- grant size;
- reporting requirements.

### 3.2 Strategic Fit

Score qualitatively by:

- mission fit;
- type of support;
- geography;
- project size;
- evidence required;
- implementation capacity.

### 3.3 Operational Fit

Check:

- customs;
- freight;
- maintenance;
- local installation;
- replacement cost;
- staff capacity.

## 4. Donor Research Record

### 4.1 Fields

For each donor track:

- organisation;
- country;
- website;
- programme;
- support type;
- eligibility;
- deadline;
- contact;
- application link;
- shipping responsibility;
- reporting requirements;
- status;
- next action;
- notes.

## 5. Outreach Package

### 5.1 Core Package

Prepare:

- one-page library profile;
- verified needs list;
- project summary;
- official request letter;
- photos with permission;
- website/project link;
- responsible contact;
- evidence of current demand.

### 5.2 Tailoring

Do not send the same generic request to every organisation.

Tailor:

- requested support;
- project size;
- language;
- evidence;
- reporting offer;
- logistics plan.

## 6. Book Donation Request Strategy

### 6.1 Exact Titles

Provide exact titles when important.

### 6.2 Category Alternatives

Also provide:

- subject;
- age/education level;
- language;
- quantity;
- acceptable alternatives.

### 6.3 Current and Future Needs

Separate:

- current reader demand;
- O/L/A/L needs;
- future curriculum;
- durable long-life reference resources.

## 7. Equipment Request Strategy

### 7.1 Minimum Specification

Define practical minimum specifications before requesting equipment.

### 7.2 Sustainability

Explain:

- intended use;
- maintenance;
- power;
- internet;
- staff management.

### 7.3 Avoid Unusable Donations

Do not accept obsolete equipment simply because it is free.

## 8. Outreach Sequence

### 8.1 Initial Contact

Short email:

- who the library serves;
- verified need;
- why the donor is a fit;
- project link;
- request for next step.

### 8.2 Application

Submit full application only after confirming fit.

### 8.3 Follow-Up

Recommended:

- one polite follow-up after a reasonable period;
- second follow-up only where appropriate;
- stop repeated contact if the organisation declines.

## 9. Outreach Status

### 9.1 Suggested Values

- Researching
- Qualified
- Not Eligible
- Ready to Contact
- Contacted
- Application Submitted
- Follow-Up Due
- Under Review
- Declined
- Approved
- Closed

## 10. Donor Conversion Funnel

### 10.1 Funnel

Track:

- qualified donors;
- contacts sent;
- responses;
- applications;
- approvals;
- fulfilled support.

### 10.2 Purpose

Use the funnel to improve strategy, not to pressure donors.

## 11. Website Role

### 11.1 Trust

Website should prove:

- verified needs;
- clear quantities;
- public progress;
- official contact;
- completed impact.

### 11.2 Conversion

Each need/project page should end with a clear contact path.

### 11.3 Transparency

A potential donor should see what is already pledged and what remains.

## 12. Recognition

### 12.1 Supporter Acknowledgement

Offer factual acknowledgement where approved.

### 12.2 No Commercial Promise

Do not promise advertising or endorsement in exchange for support unless separately approved.

## 13. Reporting

### 13.1 After Support

Provide donor with:

- confirmation;
- photos where approved;
- quantity received;
- deployment/cataloguing status;
- short impact note.

### 13.2 Website Update

Update the public tracker after verification.

## 14. Research Priorities

### 14.1 First Wave

Focus on high-fit organisations with direct public-library, literacy, book, education, or digital-access programmes.

### 14.2 Second Wave

Expand to:

- foundations;
- CSR programmes;
- technology firms;
- community associations.

## 15. Success Measures

### 15.1 Track

- qualified organisations;
- response rate;
- applications submitted;
- approvals;
- total verified support;
- average time from contact to outcome;
- repeat partners.
